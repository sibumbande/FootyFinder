SUMMARY OF CHANGES IN VERSION 2.4
This summary explains what changed from version 2.3. It does not replace the full Terms below. This is a material change: you will be asked to accept version 2.4 before you can continue to create, join or pay for matches or manage a Team.
•	A FootyFinder Referee on every match. Every match, including every Team Match, has a Referee appointed by FootyFinder. A Referee may also play in a match they referee. The Referee’s name is shown on the match page (clauses 2 and 17.5).
•	No Referee, no match. A match goes ahead only if a FootyFinder Referee is assigned to it 30 minutes before kick-off, as well as the existing go/no-go conditions. If no Referee is assigned, the match is cancelled automatically and everyone is refunded in full, exactly as for any other go/no-go cancellation (clauses 11.5, 12.18 and 15.1).
•	What Referees can see. The Referee of a match can see the display names, positions and sides of the players in that match, but not their contact details, identity document, date of birth, emergency contact or payment information (clause 8.3).
•	The Referee’s result is final. After the match the Referee records the score, who scored and who assisted each goal, and which players did not play. That result is final. Only FootyFinder can correct a clear recording error, and every correction is recorded with its reason. Hosts and Captains no longer record or confirm results (clauses 17.6 to 17.8).
•	Your version and reporting a problem. A Team’s owner or Captains, or the Host of a match, may send their own version of the result within 24 hours after the match ends. It helps FootyFinder if the Referee does not record the result, but it never changes the Referee’s result. Results can no longer be disputed; instead, a Captain or Host may report a problem within 24 hours of the result, and FootyFinder reviews it (clauses 17.9 and 17.10).
•	Own goals. An own goal counts for the team it was scored for, not for any player (clause 17.6).

SUMMARY OF CHANGES IN VERSION 2.3
This summary explains what changed from version 2.2. It does not replace the full Terms below. This is a material change: you will be asked to accept version 2.3 before you can continue to create, join or pay for matches or manage a Team.
•	Team matches. A Team owner or Captain can create a team match, either from the Team page or by choosing to play as their Team when creating a match. Team matches are always public. The creator chooses who can take the other side: “Teams only” or “Open to both” (another Team or individual players) (clauses 12.9 and 12.10).
•	Team match fee. Each Team pays R80 for every starting position in the format plus every substitute that Team chooses. For example, 11-a-side with 3 substitutes costs R880 + R240 = R1,120. FootyFinder sets the R80 (clause 12.11).
•	Publishing. To publish a team match, the Team Wallet’s available balance must cover the home Team’s fee (nothing is taken at that point), and a Team can have at most two published matches waiting for an opponent (clause 12.12).
•	Taking the other side. There is no approval step: the other side is taken straight away by whoever comes first. Another Team loads its whole squad with one button and chooses its own substitutes (and so its own team fee). In an “Open to both” match, individual players can join instead and pay R80 each, like any match. Once a player has joined, only players can join that side. Nobody can play against their own Team (clause 12.14).
•	Withdrawing. The Team that took the other side can withdraw only itself, until 30 minutes before kick-off. Its held money goes back to its Team Wallet and the side opens again (clause 12.15).
•	Who can do what. A Team’s owner and Captains act for their own Team only. Only the home Team’s owner or Captains can cancel a team match, until 30 minutes before kick-off; cancelling ends it for both sides with everything released or refunded (clauses 12.2 and 12.16).
•	Fill meter. Once the other side is taken, each Team sees its own fill meter, for example “R0 / R1,120”, and its owner or Captains fill it from the Team Wallet. The money is held, not spent: it is taken only if the match goes ahead (clause 12.17).
•	Go/no-go for team matches. 30 minutes before kick-off a team match goes ahead only if both fill meters are full, or, where players took the other side, the home Team’s meter is full and every starting position is claimed. Otherwise it is cancelled, held money goes back to each Team Wallet and every player gets their R80 back (clause 12.18).
•	Changing substitutes. A Team can change its number of substitutes until 30 minutes before kick-off; its fee is recalculated and any money held above the new fee goes back to the Team Wallet straight away (clause 12.19).
•	No opponent. A “Teams only” match that no Team has taken 24 hours before kick-off is cancelled, and nothing is owed. The home Team is warned 48 hours before kick-off (clause 12.13).
•	Topping up a Team Wallet. Any member of a Team can add money to the Team Treasury (the Team Wallet) from their own Wallet: R10 to R5,000 per contribution, and at most R5,000 and ten contributions per Team in any 24 hours (clause 12.3).
•	Taking back your unspent money. The Team spends the oldest contributions first. You can return your own unspent contributions to your Wallet at any time, even after you leave the Team, except money that is held for a team match (clause 12.4).
•	Closing a Team. When a Team is closed, or its owner and Captains cannot be contacted for sixty (60) days, each member's own unspent contributions go back to that member's Wallet. There is no proportional or equal-share split and no cash withdrawal (clause 12.5).

SUMMARY OF CHANGES IN VERSION 2.1
This summary explains what changed from version 2.0. It does not replace the full Terms below.
•	Fixed fee. Every player pays a fixed Slot Fee of R80 per match, set by FootyFinder and taken from the Wallet on joining. A Host cannot set or change the fee (clauses 2, 11 and 14).

•	Lobby lock. The Lobby locks 30 minutes before kick-off: no joining, leaving, position changes or Host cancellation (clause 11.7).

•	Wallet. Refunds and cancellation credits are paid to your Wallet. The Wallet is closed-loop and cannot be withdrawn to a bank account. On closure, an unspent balance is refunded to the original payment method (clauses 14.5, 15.7 and 21).
•	Payments. Card payments are processed by Paystack. Top-ups are R50 to R5,000, and we pay the payment provider’s fees (clauses 3 and 14).
•	Chargebacks. A chargeback may make your Wallet balance negative and restrict its use until resolved (clause 14.9).
•	Identity checks. Identity verification is no longer required at registration. We may ask you to verify your age or identity at any time (clauses 4.3 and 7).
