1. HOW TO USE THIS BACKLOG
==========================

1. Apply the approved decision record before any implementation ticket that depends on it; do not reopen policy inside an implementation ticket.
2. Select exactly one READY ticket unless an explicit recorded batch exception applies.
3. Implement only that ticket, including its tests and documentation, unless a recorded gate-level batch authorization applies.
4. Stop after verification and provide a completion report.
5. Review/approve the result before selecting the next ticket.
6. Do not combine tickets because they touch the same files.
7. If a ticket reveals required work outside its scope, record a follow-up ticket instead of silently expanding scope.
8. High-risk migrations and financial changes use expand-migrate-contract and feature flags.
9. Preserve existing user changes and never rewrite committed migrations.
10. A ticket is not complete because UI or an endpoint merely exists; trace and verify the complete path in its acceptance criteria.

Suggested board states:

BACKLOG -> BLOCKED -> READY -> IN PROGRESS -> IN REVIEW -> VERIFIED -> RELEASED

Required labels:

- gate-0 through gate-9
- frontend, backend, database, realtime, payment, security, test, documentation
- risk-low, risk-medium, risk-high, risk-critical
- external-input-blocked or new-decision-required where applicable

DEC-001 - Configurable Quick Game pricing and venue guarantee
Status: APPROVED - POLICY RECORDED (2026-09-24)
Depends on: none
Policy:
- New QUICK_GAME records accept a server-validated whole-rand per-player fee from R0 through R500; the creation default is R80.
- Existing matches retain and display their stored fees. Team fixtures use DEC-014/DEC-015 instead.
- For a managed venue, the organiser guarantees the snapshotted venue cost through a wallet hold/funding obligation.
- The selected player fee cannot exceed the lower of R500 or the calculated fair share of the venue cost across configured paid capacity. It reimburses venue cost only and cannot create organiser profit.
- Every confirmed joined place, including a reserve/substitute place, pays the same snapshotted fee.
- If the organiser cancels outside the refundable venue window, player fees are fully restored and the organiser bears the remaining non-refundable venue amount after venue credit.
Blocks resolved by policy: TKT-103, TKT-304, TKT-306, TKT-603.
Superseded in part by DEC-018 (2026-09-29): the fee is no longer configurable (every Quick Match place is a platform-fixed R80) and there is no organiser venue guarantee.

DEC-011 - Paystack personal-payment policy
Status: APPROVED - POLICY RECORDED (2026-09-24)
Depends on: none
Policy:
- Use server-initiated Paystack hosted checkout with card payments only at launch and authoritative authenticated webhooks; client redirect/success never credits a wallet.
- Deposits are ZAR R50 through R5,000. Footy Finder absorbs provider fees. Wallet withdrawals are not supported.
- Refund to the original provider method. If that fails/is unavailable, keep a pending/failed external-refund state for manual finance review; do not silently issue wallet/promotional credit.
- Chargebacks append an auditable reversal even if the personal wallet becomes negative and restrict spending until repayment/admin resolution.
- Platform Operations owns production credentials/webhooks; developers use scoped test credentials.
Blocks resolved by policy: TKT-603 through TKT-606.
CEO decisions recorded for Gate 6 (Sibu, 2026-09-29):
- D2. A chargeback is reversed when the dispute opens (the wallet may go negative and spending is restricted); if the dispute is resolved in FootyFinder's favour ("declined"), the reversed credit is restored. ToS 14.9 says so (v2.2).
- D3. A failed card refund stays FAILED for finance; an admin may retry it or explicitly return it to the wallet with a written reason (audited). It is never re-credited automatically.
- D5. The database allows a negative wallet balance only while spending is restricted; only the chargeback reversal path may go below zero.
- D6. The restriction lifts automatically when the balance is back at or above zero and no dispute is open, or by an audited admin action on a wallet that is not below zero.
- D7. Card refunds are admin-initiated only; players ask through support.
- D11 (amended by the CEO). The wallet is credited only after FootyFinder's server verifies the payment with Paystack (status success, exact amount, ZAR, card, the player's own reference). The verified webhook, the expiry job and the player's return-page status check all use the same idempotent, row-locked credit path, so a top-up is credited once whatever the order. The browser never credits on its own say-so. Only mismatches, or a success reported after the top-up was closed, go to finance REVIEW.
- Top-up quick picks: R80, R160 (default), R240, R400, plus a custom amount (R50-R5,000).
- D12. Terms of Service v2.2 (non-material clarifications): top-ups credited only once Paystack confirms (14.5), restore on a won dispute (14.9), failed card refunds never silently become wallet credit (15.7).

Depends on: DEC-005, DEC-011
Policy:
- Create at most one payable with each eligible reservation, but mark it due only after kickoff if the reservation was not cancelled.
- Settle due payables weekly. One finance/admin actor prepares and a different MFA-verified actor approves/marks paid.
- Store encrypted beneficiary details, adjustments, evidence/reference, actors, and timestamps; player APIs/logs never expose sensitive values.
- A venue beneficiary does not require a Footy Finder account and may be linked later.
- Apply refunds/adjustments before an unpaid settlement or against the next settlement; unmatched exceptions remain visible for finance resolution.
- Venue/platform cancellation fully reverses the obligation according to the snapshotted policy.
Blocks resolved by policy: TKT-607 through TKT-609, subject to DEC-005 external data.
Applied with DEC-018 and CEO decisions for Gate 6 (Sibu, 2026-09-29):
- D1. The payable is created at kickoff (not at T-30), DUE immediately, in the same transaction that starts the match: exactly once per reservation, from the admin-only priceCentsSnapshot, only for a confirmed go/no-go Quick Match (reservation CONFIRMED, confirmedAt set). A T-30 auto-cancelled or host-cancelled match never kicks off and never creates a payable; a database trigger refuses any ineligible payable.
- D4. A legacy match (no goNoGoAt/confirmedAt) that reaches kickoff gets no payable; reconciliation lists it as LEGACY_RESERVATION_UNSETTLED for manual finance handling.
- D8. Venue bank details are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM, VENUE_BENEFICIARY_ENCRYPTION_KEY), masked everywhere, usable only after a second admin approves them, and revealed in full only through an audited call.
- D9. Any two different MFA-verified platform admins (no separate finance role): the approver and the payer must differ from the preparer; approve and mark-paid need an MFA verification from the last 15 minutes.
- D10. One batch per venue per week, Monday 00:00 to Monday 00:00 Africa/Johannesburg, prepared only after the week has ended.
- "Created with the reservation" in the policy above is superseded by D1 and DEC-018 ("only a match that went ahead"), so no payable exists for a cancelled match and nothing needs reversing.

DEC-016 - Result confirmation, corrections, and statistics
Status: APPROVED - POLICY RECORDED (2026-09-24)
Depends on: DEC-015
Policy:
- HOME owner/captain proposes; AWAY owner/captain confirms or rejects within 48 hours. The proposer cannot self-confirm as the opponent.

- Statistics count confirmed/admin-final results and adjudicated forfeits. Exclude cancelled and unresolved/disputed matches. A forfeit counts match/win/loss but does not infer goals/scorers.

DEC-018 - Fixed R80 fee, hidden venue costs, no host guarantee, and T-30 go/no-go

CEO DECISION (Sibu): replace gate 3's host-set fee / venue-price model with a fixed-fee
model. Implement all of the following:

1. Fixed fee: every player who joins a Quick Match pays exactly R80 (8000 cents),
   including subs. The platform sets it. Hosts cannot set or change a fee: remove the fee
   input and the "maximum entry fee" logic from create-match (web + API validation).

   Grep all player/host DTOs and mappers to prove it.
3. No host guarantee: remove or disable gate 3's organizer guarantee (the host wallet
   hold / organizerGuaranteeCents flow). The host pays R80 like any other player if they
   play.
4. Go/no-go at T-30 minutes: a match goes ahead only if EVERY formation position on the
   pitch is filled (claimed) by 30 minutes before kickoff. Subs are optional.
   Example: kickoff 30 Oct 2026 14:00 → check at 13:30.
   - If not full at T-30: auto-cancel the match, notify every joined player, and refund

   - Implement the T-30 check as a DurableJob (existing queue, FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED

   - If full at T-30: the match is confirmed.

5. Player messaging: on the match page, show clearly "This match goes ahead only if all
   positions are filled by <T-30 time>. Otherwise it's cancelled and your R80 is
   refunded to your wallet." Show a live "X of Y positions filled".
6. Venue settlement: venues are paid AFTER the match, only for matches that went ahead.
   Do not build venue payouts now, but make sure a cancelled match creates no amount
   owed to the venue, and describe how a completed match would be marked for payout.
7. Venue cost reference (what FootyFinder pays field owners per match; admin-only data):

Follow-up decisions approved by the CEO (Sibu) on 2026-09-29 (D1-D5, as recommended in the plan):
- D1. From T-30 to kickoff the lobby is frozen: the server rejects joins, leaves, position claims, team changes, organiser formation moves and host cancellation (409 LINEUP_LOCKED). A player who cannot attend after T-30 is a no-show with no refund. This makes the T-30 decision depend only on the T-30 lineup, even if the job runs late.
- D2. A player leaving before T-30: the match stays open, the vacated position becomes claimable, and the existing credit rule is unchanged: more than 12 hours before kickoff, full R80 credit; 12 hours or less, no credit unless a paid replacement joins the same team. - D5. Player pooled field bookings (/bookings) are retired: create, contribute and the /bookings/fields catalogue return 410 PLAYER_FIELD_BOOKING_RETIRED; booking history stays readable without any money fields. DEC-020 - FootyFinder referee on every match; the referee's result is final

Decisions already made (verbatim):

Further decisions D10-D26 (approved as written on 2026-09-30, "Everything else D10-D26 is approved as written"):
- D10 Who submits the "captain" version? Team matches: the owner/captains of each team side. Quick Matches: the host. Individuals' side of an "Open to both" match: no one (it has no captain).
- D11 When can captains submit? From the end of the match until 24h after the match ends. They may resubmit; the latest submission is used, and earlier ones are kept. Submissions are hidden from the other side and from players, and only admins see them.
- D12 When can the referee submit? From kickoff (in case a game ends early) until an admin enters the result. The 2h alert doesn't lock the referee out. Once final, only an admin can change it.
- D13 What outcomes can the referee record? Played (score and goals); Forfeit (a side didn't turn up; winner recorded, no goals; counts as a W/L); Abandoned (no result; excluded from stats, no reviews). No money changes for any outcome. Fees stay as today once a match has kicked off.
- D14 Should the referee mark players who didn't turn up? Yes, for stats only. On the result form every lineup player is ticked "played" by default, and the referee can untick a player who didn't play. Unticked players get no "match played" and can't review. No penalties or no-show charges come from this (that would be a new legal commitment).
- D15 Do subs count as "played"? Yes, if they are in the kickoff lineup and not unticked by the referee.
- D16 Can a referee decline an assignment? Yes, from their Referee view until T-30. The match goes back to "Unassigned" and admins are alerted immediately. Referees can't pick matches themselves.
- D17 (REVERSED by the CEO, see below) Can the referee also be a player in the same match? As proposed: No. D9's "no conflict rule" stays, but you can't referee and play at once. Assigning someone who is in the lineup is refused, and a referee can't join a match they referee.
- D18 What do players see about the referee? The referee's display name on the match page once assigned. Referees see players' display names, positions and sides only: no contact details, no money, no venue costs.
- D19 Notifications. Referee assigned, changed or removed: in-app and email to the referee. Result final or corrected: in-app only, to everyone in the lineup record and the teams' members. Admin alerts: in-app and email to all admins. Problem report resolved: in-app only to the reporter.
- D20 Old self-reported results (before Gate 8). They are kept as they are, and still count in stats as today. Matches that haven't reached T-30 when Gate 8 ships need a referee (we're pre-launch, so there's no grandfathering).
- D21 The old "Dispute result" feature. Players can no longer open new result disputes; "Report a problem" replaces them. Existing open result disputes stay in the admin queue until closed. Booking disputes are unchanged.
- D22 Does an open problem report hide the result from stats? No. The referee's result is final (D5). Stats change only if an admin corrects the result.
- D23 Which reason is given when several go/no-go checks fail? The players' own reason first (positions unfilled or team fees unfunded). "No referee" is given only when that was the sole problem. The refunds are identical either way.
- D24 Who can write team reviews (DEC-017)? Players in the kickoff lineup record who played (D14), reviewing the opposing team, once the result is final and the outcome was Played or Forfeit. The individuals' side of an "Open to both" match can review the HOME team. HOME can't review individuals, because there's no team to review. The 7-day edit window starts from the final result.
- D25 Admin safety. Granting or removing a referee, admin result entry and corrections all need a fresh MFA check (15 min), a written reason for entries and corrections, and an audit log entry.
- D26 ToS v2.4 materiality. Material, re-acceptance required, with at least 14 days' notice (clause 26.2). It adds a new condition for a match going ahead (a referee), a new cancellation reason, final results, stats on profiles, and referees seeing player names.
