1. HOW TO USE THIS BACKLOG
==========================

1. Apply the approved decision record before any implementation ticket that depends on it; do not reopen policy inside an implementation ticket.
2. Select exactly one READY ticket unless an explicit recorded batch exception applies.
3. Implement only that ticket, including its tests and documentation. The recorded Gate 1 and Gate 2 batches are the only exceptions.
4. Stop after verification and provide a completion report.
5. Review/approve the result before selecting the next ticket.
6. Do not combine tickets because they touch the same files.
7. If a ticket reveals required work outside its scope, record a follow-up ticket instead of silently expanding scope.
8. High-risk migrations and financial changes use expand-migrate-contract and feature flags.
9. Preserve existing user changes and never rewrite committed migrations.
10. A ticket is not complete because UI or an endpoint merely exists; trace and verify the complete path in its acceptance criteria.

Suggested board states:

BACKLOG -> BLOCKED -> READY -> IN PROGRESS -> IN REVIEW -> VERIFIED -> RELEASED

Required labels:

- gate-0 through gate-9
- frontend, backend, database, realtime, payment, security, test, documentation
- risk-low, risk-medium, risk-high, risk-critical
- external-input-blocked or new-decision-required where applicable

DEC-001 - Configurable Quick Game pricing and venue guarantee
Status: APPROVED - POLICY RECORDED (2026-09-24)
Depends on: none
Policy:
- New QUICK_GAME records accept a server-validated whole-rand per-player fee from R0 through R500; the creation default is R80.
- Existing matches retain and display their stored fees. Team fixtures use DEC-014/DEC-015 instead.
- For a managed venue, the organiser guarantees the snapshotted venue cost through a wallet hold/funding obligation.
- The selected player fee cannot exceed the lower of R500 or the calculated fair share of the venue cost across configured paid capacity. It reimburses venue cost only and cannot create organiser profit.
- Every confirmed joined place, including a reserve/substitute place, pays the same snapshotted fee.
- If the organiser cancels outside the refundable venue window, player fees are fully restored and the organiser bears the remaining non-refundable venue amount after venue credit.
Blocks resolved by policy: TKT-103, TKT-304, TKT-306, TKT-603.

DEC-002 - Sixty-minute transition policy
Status: APPROVED - POLICY RECORDED (2026-09-24)
Depends on: none
Policy:
- Every newly created 5/7/11-a-side match uses 60 minutes.
- A one-time idempotent, audited operation converts every future not-started 90-minute match to 60 minutes, retaining fee, participants, and other facts.
- Notify affected organisers and participants after commit.
- Active, completed, cancelled, and other historical records remain unchanged.

DEC-014 - Team-wallet governance
Status: APPROVED - POLICY RECORDED (2026-09-24)
Depends on: DEC-011
Policy:
- Use separate TeamWalletAccount/transaction/hold aggregates with an immutable, derived, non-negative balance; never model the team as an owner's subaccount.
- Owner and active captains may spend without quorum. The owner must transfer ownership before leaving; demoted/removed captains lose authority immediately.
- Contributions are idempotent personal-to-team transfers from R10 through R5,000 and cannot exceed available personal balance; apply daily abuse limits.
- The contributor may self-refund their own uncommitted provenance. Exceptional refunds require review and always return to original provenance.
- Current members see balance, transaction amount/type/time, and contributor display name. Only owner/captains see actionable holds; only platform admins see provider/internal references.
- Block closure while balance, holds, or obligations remain. Settle/release/refund to zero, archive the team, and retain immutable audit/financial history.
Blocks resolved by policy: TKT-701 through TKT-703, TKT-709.

DEC-015 - Challenge lifecycle, authority, and funding
Status: APPROVED - POLICY RECORDED (2026-09-24)
Depends on: DEC-014
Policy:
- Owner and active captains may create, withdraw, accept, or decline for their own team/side; record every actor and reject former/wrong-side actors.
- Eligible teams must match the fixture format and other server eligibility rules. Multiple pending challenges are allowed.
- A challenge expires at the earlier of 48 hours after creation or 24 hours before kickoff. The challenger may withdraw while pending.
- The first atomic acceptance attaches AWAY and cancels all competing pending challenges.
- Venue cost is fixed 50/50; assign an odd cent deterministically to HOME. HOME holds its share at publication and AWAY holds its share atomically on acceptance. Capture after reservation and matchup confirmation.
- A unilateral cancelling side bears the non-refundable venue amount; mutual cancellation splits it equally; venue/platform cancellation releases both sides fully.
- Retain private zero-cost manual-venue planning fixtures, but exclude them from public discovery, challenge, settlement, and funded-ready states.
Blocks resolved by policy: TKT-704 through TKT-709.

DEC-016 - Result confirmation, corrections, and statistics
Status: APPROVED - POLICY RECORDED (2026-09-24)
Depends on: DEC-015
Policy:
- HOME owner/captain proposes; AWAY owner/captain confirms or rejects within 48 hours. The proposer cannot self-confirm as the opponent.
- Replacing a pending proposal creates a revision and restarts the deadline. Scorers finalize with the result and do not require individual confirmation.
- Rejection or timeout opens/admin-escalates the dispute path and never silently finalizes.
- A final result changes only through an append-only reasoned admin revision in the dispute workflow; recompute projections idempotently.
- Statistics count confirmed/admin-final results and adjudicated forfeits. Exclude cancelled and unresolved/disputed matches. A forfeit counts match/win/loss but does not infer goals/scorers.
Blocks resolved by policy: TKT-801 through TKT-804 and TKT-211 semantics.

3A. CROSS-CUTTING APPROVED PRODUCT/UX RULES
============================================

- Navigation: put My Profile, Wallet, and Sign out in the authenticated header/avatar account menu. Keep Matches and Teams primary; add Social as a primary area when Gate 9 ships.
- Preferred positions: allow up to four unique ordered values; the first is primary.
- Onboarding order: credentials -> verify email -> DOB/city/experience/positions/photo -> current legal acceptance -> activate profile.
- Experience: required whole years from 0 through 60, user-editable and visible only on authenticated profiles.
- DOB: store the full date for age enforcement; expose only to self and tightly authorized admins, never other players/anonymous DTOs.
- Canonical URLs: immutable opaque public-match slug; frozen human-readable venue slug with permanent redirect aliases when needed; UUIDs/private tokens are not public canonical identifiers.
- Sharing/QR: canonical URLs only, with no user referral identifier or campaign tracking in the initial implementation.
- Public-share E2E funding: use the environment-gated demo-deposit API with unique idempotency keys and disposable cleanup; do not write browser-test balances directly.
- Operational email: send email only for challenge received/accepted/expiring, result confirmation/dispute, payment/refund failure, and account-security events. Chat never sends email.
- Alert owner: Platform Operations. Page payment/security/reconciliation failures immediately; review ordinary product-flow alerts daily.
- Friendship: authenticated minimal-card search by username/name/city, preferences/rate limits, one pending request per pair, 30-day expiry, 20 new requests/day, 100 pending outgoing maximum, and an option to disable incoming requests.
- Blocking: bilateral discovery/social invisibility and no requests/DMs/targeted invites/notifications. Shared teams/matches keep only the operational identity required by roster/lineup/result; blocking does not silently remove either user.
- Team chat: one permanent team-owned conversation. Current members can read/send retained history; removed members immediately lose all access. Generate one first-unread in-app notification, suppress self/duplicates until read, and do not email chat.
- Advanced escrow: TKT-906 is approved only after Gate 7 reconciliation is proven. Use refundable personal-wallet holds toward a team target, reject overfunding, advance only when both targets fund before expiry, capture successful obligations, and release on expiry/cancellation except approved charges.
- Advanced player statistics: TKT-907 remains deferred; do not add attendance, assists, POTM, player ratings, or captaincy history without a new metric/capture/correction decision.
